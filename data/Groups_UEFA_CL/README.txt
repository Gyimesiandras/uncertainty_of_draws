- Draws based on the datasets in data/Groups_UEFA_CL
- ClubElo is derived from clubelo.com. The ELO column is different and not used in the analysis.
- Group is the group the club was drawn in that particular season 
- Pot is the pot that club was present in
- First 32 teams: teams present in the group stage of that season 
- Last 4 teams: 
  -2 teams of 2 highest UEFA country coefficients of previous season were added. 
  -1 team from Championship path and 1 from other path were added, the teams who were closest to make it were added (lowest aggregated goal difference). In case of tie: broken randomly.
  -If team was already chosen because of highest UEFA country coefficient, we pick the second best team 
  -No team from the added paths resulted in more than 5 teams from the same association
  -No 5 teams of the same association were present in the same pot of 9 teams
  -Additional teams can be recognized because there pot and group are -1

- Draw_group_stage_real: new groups are formed based on the real pots
- Draw_group_stage_elo: teams are sorted based on elo, first 8 go into pot 1, next 8 go in pot 2, and so on. Then, based on these pots, groups are formed
- Draw_iRR_real: teams are sorted based on UEFA club coefficients of previous season, CL_winner previous year + 8 next first teams go into pot 1, next 9 teams in pot 2, and so on.
- Draw_iRR_elo: teams are ordered based on elo
