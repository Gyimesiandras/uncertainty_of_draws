# Content

data_2019-2026.xlsx contains data on Champions League (CL) participants from seasons 2019/20 to 2025/26 under both the group stage and the post-2024 iRR qualification rules.
- Club: the id of the team
- Elo: the Elo rating of the team on 1st September right before the corresponding season
- Pot: the Pot number of the team between 1 to 4 under the group stage format. -1 means the team did not qualify under the group stage format, but would qualify under the iRR format.

Data_draws_2019-2026.zip contains the generated draws for the simulations under four different format/seeding:
- Draw_group_stage_real contains 1000 draws of the group stage format with the real seeding. "Club" ids are used as identifiers
